A simple software system is not necessarily a boring one. In practice, the most durable systems are the ones that make their constraints obvious and keep the maintenance effort low.

## Small design decisions matter

When the structure is clear, it is easier to add new content without rethinking the whole site. Static output helps here because the edits stay readable and the deployment stays cheap.

## Favor clarity over cleverness

I try to keep content models simple and layout primitives reusable. That reduces the chance of surprise during updates and makes the project easier to hand off later.

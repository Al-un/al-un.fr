I’m launching this site as a place to collect projects, writing, and experiments. The goal is to keep the experience lightweight, maintainable, and easy to extend over time.

## Why this exists

The site acts as a personal home for my work and interests. It gives me a single place to share writing, software experiments, and little game prototypes without bringing in a heavier CMS or backend.

## What comes next

I plan to keep the system static, document-heavy, and intentionally simple. That makes it easy to update from GitHub and scale without unnecessary infrastructure.
